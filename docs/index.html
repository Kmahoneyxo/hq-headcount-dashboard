How to read this

  • Ideal HC — assigned accounts ÷ ideal book size for that country × segment
  • Hire — under-staffed vs model with positive growth in ideal book range
  • Optimize — over-staffed; consolidate/redistribute books before net-new hires
  • Hold — within ±10% of ideal HC
  • Do Not Hire — market growth ≤ 0 in ideal book range

FY26 score methodology

  • Source: sales_book_summary_v2 (Book Building FY26) — same flags as Tableau workbook
  • Rep score: AVG of 10 account flags per parent × rep; range −1 to +1
  • % book built: share of flags with value > 0 (shown in table)
  • In this dash: market-level average across reps in segment (sql/11, joined in query 16)
  • Status: methodology implemented; policy target % still TBD (Addy). Chart compares FY26 % built vs current book as % of data-derived ideal book — they are independent signals.
