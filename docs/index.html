How to read this

  • Ideal HC — assigned accounts ÷ ideal book size for that country × segment
  • Ideal / optimal PCID — accounts per rep at the revenue-growth plateau (perfect book)
  • Segment avg PQR — prior-quarter revenue per rep; benchmark for book weight
  • Hire — under-staffed vs model with positive growth in ideal book range
  • Optimize — over-staffed; consolidate/redistribute books before net-new hires
  • Hold — within ±10% of ideal HC
  • Do Not Hire — market growth ≤ 0 in ideal book range
  • Impact coverage — median impact calls per assigned account (90d); shown in market lookup
  • SBS whitespace — unassigned accounts in country (assignable to reps); books_buildable = whitespace ÷ ideal PCID
  • Segment — GTM sales segment from team name (M, UMM, ACC, L, NAM, DCA)

HQ methodology — two layers (Layer 1 → Layer 2)

This dashboard implements the HQ capacity model in two layers. Layer 1 (book health) confirms ideal book size and rep-level health before any staffing action. Layer 2 (headcount) applies that book size to total assigned accounts to derive optimal headcount and Hire / Hold / Optimize recommendations.

LayerQuestionDashboard signalssql/16 fields
1 — Book health What is the ideal book? Is each rep's book healthy? Perfect book, PQR, impact coverage, FY26 score, too big / too little ideal_pcid, median_impact_calls_per_account, fy26_pct_book_built, reps_too_big, reps_too_little, reps_healthy
2 — Headcount How many reps should this market carry? Optimal HC, gap, recommendation, SBS routing ideal_headcount, headcount_gap, recommended_action, sbs_whitespace, books_buildable_from_sbs

Build sequence: validate ideal book size (Layer 1) → compute optimal headcount (Layer 2) → split / grow books only after Layer 1 is trusted. Do not act on HC recommendations where growth curve is flat or sample size is too small.

Why these numbers? — PQR vs PCID methodology

PCID (parent company IDs per rep) is book size — how many accounts a rep carries.

PQR (prior-quarter revenue) is book weight — how much revenue those accounts generated in the prior 90 days.

Optimal / perfect book — sql/16 buckets reps by PCID (1–10, 11–20, … up to 150). For each bucket we take median revenue growth (current 90d vs prior 90d). The optimal bucket is the largest size where growth stays within 85% of the segment peak and adding accounts no longer improves growth. That midpoint becomes ideal PCID for headcount: assigned accounts ÷ ideal PCID = ideal headcount.

Revenue growth — (current 90d revenue − prior 90d PQR) ÷ prior PQR, per rep then aggregated by PCID bucket. Comparison windows: current 20260427–20260725 vs prior PQR 20260128–20260426 (quarterly business rule). Reps with prior PQR < $5K are excluded. Growth is capped at −50% to +100%. The market lookup shows median growth by bucket and when growth falls as books exceed the coverage inflection (~100 PCIDs for many segments).

Too big — When avg book exceeds the coverage inflection point and median impact calls/account drop below 90% of peak, or when individual reps exceed segment avg PCID/PQR with weak coverage or revenue below PQR. Growth curve + coverage inflection together define when books are too large.

PQR vs PCID tradeoff — A rep can be “too big” with high PCID or high PQR vs segment averages if coverage (impact calls/account) drops below 90% of segment norm or current revenue falls below PQR. Larger books spread coverage thin; the model favors the growth-optimal PCID, not simply the highest account count.

Impact coverage — Sum of impact_calls from rep_activity_sales over the trailing 90 days, divided by assigned PCIDs per rep (impact_calls_per_account). The dashboard shows the market median (median_impact_calls_per_account). Coverage peaks at a book size (inflection point); status is Declining when avg book exceeds that size and median coverage falls below 90% of peak.

SBS whitespace — Unassigned parent company IDs in a country (team None on JAM). These are assignable accounts reps could grow into. books_buildable_from_sbs = whitespace ÷ ideal PCID. SBS is country-level; all segment rows in a country share the same pool.

Supporting signals — Opp pipeline plateau (rev/job peaks at a book size) and coverage inflection (impact calls/account peak) are shown per market when selecting a country × segment.

Healthy book — A rep has a healthy book when they are not flagged too_big or too_little (sql/16–17). Checklist per segment: PCID within ±10% of ideal (and not below ideal), PQR at or above segment benchmark, impact coverage ≥ 90% of segment average, current revenue ≥ prior-quarter PQR. The market lookup shows segment thresholds and % of reps meeting this definition (from rep_book.json).

FY26 score methodology

  • Source: sales_book_summary_v2 (Book Building FY26) — same flags as Tableau workbook
  • Rep score: AVG of 10 account flags per parent × rep; range −1 to +1
  • % book built: share of flags with value > 0 (shown in table)
  • In this dash: market-level average across reps in segment (sql/11, joined in query 16)
  • Status: methodology implemented; policy target % still TBD (Addy). Chart compares FY26 % built vs current book as % of data-derived ideal book — they are independent signals.
