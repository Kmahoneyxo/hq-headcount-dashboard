HQ methodology

Official reference (Indeed SSO): Connect PTD — book & headcount methodology

This dashboard implements the HQ capacity model in two layers. Layer 1 (book health) confirms ideal book size and rep-level health before any staffing action. Layer 2 (headcount) applies that book size to total assigned accounts to derive optimal headcount and Hire / Hold / Optimize recommendations.

LayerQuestionDashboard signalssql/16 fields
1 — Book health What is the ideal book? Is each rep's book healthy? Perfect book, PQR, impact coverage, FY26 score, too big / too little ideal_pcid, reps_too_big, reps_too_little, perfect_book_source
2 — Headcount How many reps should this market carry? Optimal HC, gap, recommendation, SBS routing ideal_headcount, headcount_gap, recommended_action, sbs_whitespace

Build sequence: validate ideal book size (Layer 1) → compute optimal headcount (Layer 2) → split / grow books only after Layer 1 is trusted. Small segments use relaxed curve (≥5 reps/bucket), peer-segment ideal, or segment median when strict curve (≥20 reps/bucket) cannot compute — see perfect_book_source.

JV ($/job) vs book size — job value plateau

Job value (JV) — current 90d revenue ÷ job count (agg_job_id from JAM, sql/19). Reps are bucketed by PCID using the same bands as the revenue growth curve (1–10, 11–20, … 150+). Each bucket shows median $/job across reps with prior PQR ≥ $5K and jobs > 0.

JV plateau — largest book-size bucket where median $/job stays within 90% of the segment peak and the next larger bucket shows lower $/job (sql/16 opp_plateau). Fields: jv_plateau_book_max, jv_plateau_rev_per_job. When avg book exceeds the plateau (~95%), opp pipeline status is Plateaued — little marginal $/job gain from bigger books.

Leadership signals: segment median JV vs plateau $/job; JV curve alongside impact coverage and revenue growth. HC reason may cite JV plateau when books exceed the inflection and $/job has peaked.

Revenue window: current 20260427–20260725 (same as growth curve). Bucket export: sql/21_jv_by_bucket.sql.

Why these numbers? — growth curve & perfect book

Optimal / perfect book — sql/16 buckets reps by PCID (1–10, 11–20, … up to 150). For each bucket we take median revenue growth (current 90d vs prior 90d). The optimal bucket is the largest size where growth stays within 85% of the segment peak. That midpoint becomes ideal PCID.

Small segments — when no bucket has ≥20 reps, we fall back to relaxed curve (≥5), peer-segment ideal from another country, or segment median PCID. Check perfect_book_source per market.

Revenue windows: current 20260427–20260725 vs prior PQR 20260128–20260426. Reps with prior PQR < $5K excluded from growth curve.

Too big / too little — PCID or PQR above segment avg plus weak coverage or revenue below PQR; or below ideal PCID.

FY26 book score
  • Source: sales_book_summary_v2 (Book Building FY26)
  • Rep score: AVG of account flags per parent × rep (−1 to +1)
  • % book built: share of flags with value > 0
  • Independent signal from data-derived perfect book — compare in exports, not a HC gate.

Warehouse — Trino / Quest prod

Queries run on Quest prod (Trino) against the Indeed datalake. Key tables:

  • datalake.imhotep_iceberg.jobactivitymetrics — PCIDs, revenue, jobs (agg_job_id), team assignment
  • datalake.sales_data_strategy_dsa.current_parent_rep_assignment — rep → market metadata
  • datalake.sales_data_strategy_dsa.rep_activity_sales — impact_calls (coverage)
  • datalake.sales_data_strategy_dsa.sales_book_summary_v2 — FY26 book build score
  • datalake.scss.client_attributes_dim_parent_attributes_current — SBS / unassigned parents (sql/16)

Partition windows (sql/16, sql/17 — update before refresh):

  • JAM filter: dl__yyyymmdd_cst BETWEEN '20260128' AND '20260725'
  • Current 90d revenue: 20260427–20260725
  • Prior PQR: 20260128–20260426
  • Impact calls: rep_activity_sales.date between 2026-04-27 and 2026-07-25

GTM v2 segment logic: sql/_sales_segment_v2.sql (inline in sql/16, sql/17). Country × segment from current_sales_team_name; excludes S/XL employer-size segments and Agency teams. Market rollups: DE/AT/CH → DACH, BE/NL/LU → BNL, GB → UK.

Formulas — Layer 1 & Layer 2
LayerFormula / ruleFields
1 — Ideal PCID Reps bucketed by PCID (1–10 … 150+). Median rev growth (current 90d vs prior PQR) per bucket. 85% rule: largest bucket within 85% of segment peak growth. Fallbacks: relaxed curve (≥5 reps/bucket), peer-segment ideal, segment median. ideal_pcid, perfect_book_source, growth_by_bucket
1 — Book health Too big = PCID or PQR above segment avg + weak coverage (<90% segment avg) or rev < PQR. Too little = PCID below ideal. reps_too_big, reps_too_little, pct_reps_healthy
2 — Headcount optimal_headcount = assigned_accounts ÷ ideal_pcid. Gap = current reps − optimal. Hire / Hold / Optimize from gap thresholds. ideal_headcount, headcount_gap, headcount_recommendation
2 — Curve gating Hire/Optimize held to Hold when ideal PCID is not validated by growth curve (curve_strict or curve_relaxed with positive growth). hc_curve_validated, hc_curve_gate_reason
